# Trademarks

The code of Kryo Shard is free software under the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **names and logos are not**.

"Kryo Shard", "Kryo Studio", the ice shard logo, the Kryo Studio hexagon logo and the related visuals in [`assets/brand`](assets/brand) belong to Kryo Studio. The GPL-3.0 covers the code, not these marks (see [NOTICE](NOTICE), section 7e).

## You can

- Say that your project is "based on Kryo Shard" or "a fork of Kryo Shard".
- Use the name and logo to talk about Kryo Shard itself: articles, videos, reviews, tutorials, links to the official download.
- Package the unmodified official release for a software store or package manager, under the Kryo Shard name.

## You can't

- Distribute a modified version under the name "Kryo Shard", or with its logo.
- Use a name or logo that could be confused with Kryo Shard or Kryo Studio.
- Suggest that your project, product or service is official, approved or supported by Kryo Studio when it is not.

## If you fork

Pick your own name and logo, replace the files in `assets/brand` and `src-tauri/icons`, and keep the "Based on Kryo Shard by Kryo Studio" notice required by the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file.

Questions: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/KryoStudio/kryo-shard/issues).
